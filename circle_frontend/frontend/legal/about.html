About Circle

Circle is a community-first social platform built to help people connect, share, and grow together. Our mission is to create a safe place where real conversations can happen without the noise and distraction of traditional social media.

What we believe

We believe that meaningful connections are the foundation of healthy communities. Circle is designed to help people discover shared interests, join topic-based groups, and engage with content that matters to them.

Why Circle exists

Our platform is built around trust, privacy, and authenticity. We want users to feel confident sharing their thoughts, joining communities, and building relationships with others in a respectful environment.

How we support you

  • Transparent policies for privacy and content.
  • Tools that give you control over your experience.
  • Safety-first moderation to keep conversations constructive.
